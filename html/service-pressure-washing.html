What pressure washing does

Pressure washing uses a controlled stream of water to remove dirt and buildup from suitable outdoor surfaces. The method and pressure need to match the material: some finishes can be damaged by excessive force or improper technique, so the surface should be considered before cleaning.

Clean agreed exterior areas

Describe the surface and the areas you want cleaned when asking about a project. The job scope should identify which areas are included before work begins.

Address visible buildup

Outdoor surfaces can collect dirt, organic residue, and weather-related grime. Cleaning can improve their appearance, though results depend on the surface and the type of buildup.

Use the right approach

Different surfaces have different cleaning needs. A review of the material and its condition helps determine whether pressure washing is appropriate.

Why outdoor surfaces may need washing

Driveways, walkways, patios, and other exterior surfaces can become visibly dirty over time. A wash can be part of a seasonal property refresh or preparation for other outdoor work. It is not suitable for every material or every stain, so set expectations based on the actual surface.
