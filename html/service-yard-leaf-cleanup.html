What a yard cleanup can include

Yard cleanup focuses on collecting and clearing leaves and loose debris from the areas included in the project. Depending on the property and requested scope, that can mean refreshing lawn and landscape areas after leaves or storm debris have accumulated.

Leaf removal

Leaves can be gathered from lawn and other agreed areas to give the yard a cleaner, more maintained appearance.

Loose yard debris

Small, loose debris can be addressed as part of a cleanup when it is included in the agreed project. Describe anything unusual or bulky when you request an estimate.

Seasonal or one-time visit

Schedule a cleanup when seasonal buildup becomes difficult to keep up with, or request a one-time refresh before a project or event.

Why leaves and debris should be cleared

A thick layer of leaves can cover the lawn, hide walkways and landscape edges, and make the yard look neglected. Clearing accumulated material helps restore a tidy outdoor space and makes it easier to see the condition of the areas beneath it.

For yard and leaf cleanup in Cumming, GA, tell us where material has collected, whether it is mostly leaves or mixed debris, and whether hauling should be part of the estimate. The amount and access can affect the scope of the job.
